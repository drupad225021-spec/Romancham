Anatomical Segmentation

2. MediaPipe Face Mesh & Oral Cavity Cutout

Standard bounding box face detectors (like Haar Cascades or generic YOLO) fail because facial hair is confined to specific anatomical regions: the cheeks, chin, mustache, and jawline. Detecting hair across the entire face produces false positives on hair bangs, eyelashes, and eyebrows.

We employ Google's face_landmarker.task 468/478-point 3D Face Mesh to create a precise anatomical polygonal mask:

  • Jawline perimeter indices: [234, 93, 132, 58, 172, 136, 150, 149, 176, 148, 152, 377, 400, 378, 379, 365, 397, 288, 361, 454]
  • Upper beard & cheek margins: [454, 323, 366, 411, 427, 345, 287, 410, 322, 391, 393, 164, 167, 165, 92, 186, 57, 212, 116, 207, 137, 93, 234]
  • Oral Cavity Punch-Out: The outer lips [61, 185, 40, 39, 37, 0, 267, 269, 270, 409, 291, 375, 321, 405, 314, 17, 84, 181, 91, 146, 61] are punched out with cv2.fillPoly(beard_mask, ..., 0) so teeth, lips, and tongue reflections do not get mistaken for beard hairs. 
# Extract jawline and cheek polygon from 468 MediaPipe landmarks
poly_arr = np.array(poly_pts, dtype=np.int32)
cv2.fillPoly(beard_mask, [poly_arr], 255)

# Punch out outer lip contour to eliminate mouth/teeth artifacts
cv2.fillPoly(beard_mask, [np.array(lip_pts, dtype=np.int32)], 0)
Trichological Signal Extraction

3. Multi-Scale Black Top-Hat Transform

In dermatological trichology, hair filaments present as dark, narrow linear structures superimposed on a lighter dermal surface. A direct edge detector (like standard Sobel or Canny) triggers heavily on skin pores, skin folds, and compression artifacts.

To solve this, we compute the Black Top-Hat Transform, which is the difference between the morphological closing of an image and the input image:

TopHat(I) = (I • B) - I

By taking the maximum response between a 3x3 and a 5x5 rectangular structuring element, we isolate hair filaments having widths of 1 to 4 pixels while suppressing broader skin gradients: 

k3 = cv2.getStructuringElement(cv2.MORPH_RECT, (3, 3))
k5 = cv2.getStructuringElement(cv2.MORPH_RECT, (5, 5))
th3 = cv2.morphologyEx(gray, cv2.MORPH_BLACKHAT, k3)
th5 = cv2.morphologyEx(gray, cv2.MORPH_BLACKHAT, k5)
tophat = cv2.max(th3, th5)

# Eliminate noise by intersecting Canny edges with Black Top-Hat mask
hair_candidates = cv2.bitwise_and(canny, canny, mask=((tophat > 8) & (beard_mask > 0)).astype(np.uint8) * 255)
Topological Thinning

4. Zhang-Suen Morphological Thinning

A single beard hair in a high-resolution portrait can span 3 to 12 pixels in width. If we counted raw pixels or contours, thick beard strands would register as hundreds of distinct points.

We employ the Zhang-Suen thinning algorithm (cv2.ximgproc.thinning) to iteratively erode the binary hair candidate mask until all linear structures are reduced to 1-pixel-wide central medial axes (topological skeletons).

After skeletonization, we run cv2.connectedComponentsWithStats to analyze the length and centroids of every discrete hair filament:

  • Strands < 2px in length are rejected as camera sensor noise.
  • Strands > 1500px in length are rejected as clothing collars or scarf seams.
  • Every surviving strand's centroid determines whether it belongs to the left or right mandibular quadrant.
Zero-False-Positive Filtering

5. The Robust Beard Presence Gate

A frequent issue with facial hair algorithms is giving female portraits or clean-shaven faces non-zero follicle counts (e.g. 1,000–3,000 hairs) due to peach fuzz or camera noise.

To guarantee zero false positives, we engineered a multi-signal Beard Presence Gate:

Three Quantitative Dermal Signatures
  1. Filament Percentage (filament_pct): The fraction of pixels in the lower-face mask showing genuine Top-Hat filament activation. Smooth skin remains below 3.0%, while beards exceed 3.5% to 28%.
  2. Forehead Skin Contrast (darkness_diff): We sample baseline bare skin at the upper forehead landmarks [10, 67, 109, 338, 297] and compare it to the mean luminance of the beard ROI. Clean-shaven skin has a negligible differential (< 15), whereas beards create significant darkening (> 25 to 100+).
  3. Valid Hair Components (valid_hair_components): Connected component filament count. Clean skin produces < 4 valid components.
# Robust Multi-Parametric Beard Presence Gate
is_beard_present = (
    (filament_pct > 3.2 and tophat_mean > 1.6 and valid_hair_components >= 5) or
    (darkness_diff > 25 and (filament_pct > 1.8 or valid_hair_components >= 4)) or
    (tophat_mean > 3.0 and (valid_hair_components >= 5 or filament_pct > 4.0)) or
    (valid_hair_components >= 20 and tophat_mean > 1.5)
)

if not is_beard_present:
    # Clean-shaven face (woman, clean-shaven man, or smooth skin)
    total_hair_estimate = 0
    density_cm2 = 0.0

Metric Normalization

6. Physical Scale & Follicle Density

Camera distance varies drastically between a close-up selfie and a portrait taken from two meters away. Counting pixels without spatial normalization produces erratic results.

We calibrate physical scale using the anatomical inter-cheekbone distance (Zygomatic breadth):

  • The distance between MediaPipe landmark 234 (left zygomatic arch) and landmark 454 (right zygomatic arch) averages 135 millimeters across adult human populations.
  • We compute the Euclidean pixel distance between these points: pixel_face_width = ||pt_left - pt_right||
  • The physical scale factor is derived: mm_per_pixel = 135.0 / pixel_face_width
  • The square area per pixel in cm²: cm2_per_pixel = (mm_per_pixel / 10.0)²

This converts raw pixel areas directly into true biological surface area (typically 60 to 160 cm² for adult male lower faces) and allows calculating true follicular density in hairs per cm².

Telemetry Synthesis

7. Mandibular Symmetry & Biometric Analytics

Once hair strands are isolated, metrics_engine.py evaluates comprehensive topological data:

A

Bilateral Mandibular Symmetry

Calculates the left-to-right ratio across the sagittal facial midline (midpoint of nose tip #1 and chin #152). Reports equilibrium percentage and describes predominance (e.g. "Symmetric Euclidean Balance" or "Right-Sided Predominance").

B

Hydration & Grooming Telemetry

Estimates optimal beard oil dosage in microliters (µL) and drops (~50 µL/drop) based on follicular surface area, alongside estimated shaving duration and cartridge wear index.

C

Persona Classification

Categorizes the subject into 6 follicular archetypes: Clean Shaven (Level 0), Light Stubble (Level 1), Medium Trim (Level 2), Full Follicular Coverage (Level 3), Heavy Lumberjack (Level 4), and Maximum Volumetric Density (Level 5).

Visual Synthesis

8. Cyberpunk HUD Overlay & Turbo Density Heatmap

The backend returns two transparent 32-bit RGBA base64 masks overlaid directly on top of the portrait:

  • Glow Bloom Strands: Filtered skeleton strands dilated by 3x3 and blurred with a 7x7 Gaussian kernel to synthesize a neon bloom aura (#00e5ff cyan transitioning to #d946ef magenta).
  • Biometric HUD Wireframe: Renders landmark dots at key mandibular coordinates, chin midline tracking vector, and tech corner brackets on the bounding box.
  • Turbo Density Heatmap: Applies a 31x31 Gaussian blur to the skeleton and maps normalized follicular concentration through the scientific cv2.COLORMAP_TURBO color gradient, isolated strictly within the beard polygon.
Edge Computing

9. Live Webcam Real-Time Engine

To give users instant live feedback before locking in a high-res capture, the frontend implementation in ImageUploader.tsx runs an in-browser edge detection pipeline inside a 60 FPS requestAnimationFrame loop:

  • Captures video frames to an offscreen processing canvas at 160x120 resolution.
  • Samples the lower 50% mandibular region and computes spatial luminance gradients (Sobel approximation).
  • Renders real-time glowing strand sparks and an animated bidirectional sweeping laser scanner bar.
  • Continuously updates live hair estimates and bilateral balance ratios in real-time.
  • Clicking "Lock & Generate Full Report" takes the full-resolution uncompressed frame from the video track, sends it to FastAPI, and produces the complete multi-layer diagnostic dashboard.
